Portfolio of Evidence

Project Presentation 3: Agile practice on the KasiBook project.

The project

KasiBook

KasiBook is a digital booking and order management platform for township service businesses in Khayelitsha. It was built by a team of three for the Entrepreneurial WIL Project. My part was the backend and database connectivity, including the SQLite database. The artefacts below show how the team planned and tracked the work using Agile.

How it is organised

Collect, select, reflect, connect

The portfolio follows four steps. Each artefact is collected from the project, selected as evidence, reflected on in its own page, and connected to the rest of the portfolio.

01CollectGather the artefacts from the project.
02SelectChoose the evidence that best shows what I did.
03ReflectExplain what each artefact shows about my learning.
04ConnectLink the artefacts and reflections together.
